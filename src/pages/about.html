About us

We make small things that do their job.

Lean is a phone stand. One piece, seven ribs, and an optional slot so your charging cable can pass through the base.

The idea

Why Lean exists.

A phone stand should not make you choose between propping your phone up and charging it. So the model has a slot in the base: the cable passes through and the phone keeps charging while it leans.
