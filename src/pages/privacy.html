What this site does

[EDIT: confirm.] This site uses no third-party trackers. Your choices (model, color, options) and your bag are kept in your browser for the session only and are not sent anywhere. The one exception is the ideas board, below.

The ideas board

If you send an idea, we store the text, the name you typed (optional), and the time. To limit spam we also keep a scrambled (salted, one-way hashed) form of your IP address, never the address itself. If you vote, your browser keeps a random id in local storage and we store only a hash of it, so one browser counts once. Ideas are read before they appear, and they are public once approved, so please leave out personal details. [EDIT: how long ideas are kept, and how to ask for one to be removed.]
