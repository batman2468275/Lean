# Optional atmosphere media

Drop generated files here. **All of them are optional**: the build lists the files that exist and the site looks finished without any of them
(CSS gradients and the built-in line textures are the fallback). Never put the product, a phone, a screen, text, a logo or a person in these files.
See `docs/higgsfield-brief.md` for the prompts, models and settings.

| File | What | Where it shows | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| `leaf-veins.webp` | macro leaf veins, glowing green on near-black (21:9) | behind the Home hero, 6 percent opacity, nature level 1 and 2 | under 250 KB |
| `contours.webp` | topographic contour lines (16:9) | behind About, 6 percent opacity, nature level 1 and 2 | under 250 KB |
| `hero-plate.mp4` | 6 to 8 s dark macro loop, no audio | behind the Home hero, wide screens only, muted, looped | under 2.5 MB |
| `hero-plate-poster.webp` | first frame of the video (optional) | the poster, and what small screens, reduced motion and Save-Data see instead of the video | under 250 KB |
| `og-plate.webp` | dark studio backdrop (16:9) | background of the social image made by `tools/make_og.cjs` | under 250 KB |
